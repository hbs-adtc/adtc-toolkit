## Steps in detail

### 1. Land the goal and high-level logistics
Start with what people should leave with: knowledge, an output, or connections. Then settle:
- Topic
- Format
- Target speakers
- Funding need
- Opportunities to partner with other clubs or schools
- Rough timeframe

### 2. Find a speaker
- Check the Speaker Tracker first (link above).
- Check with the Design Club for warm intros.
- Cold outreach on LinkedIn.

### 3. Check the Academic Calendar for free time
Pick a date that avoids exams, breaks and other conflicts. Do not book a room or create a Luma until the speaker has confirmed the date.

### 4. Meet with the speaker to finalize logistics and content
Usually this takes at least two meetings to hammer out the content. Speakers do not always know what they should talk about, even when they confidently say they do. Review their slides and check for the content that will be most valuable for students.

### 5. Book a room
Default room and time slot are in the Club info tab. No other HBS approval is needed.

On my.hbs.edu:
1. Reserve a Room
2. Create a Reservation
3. Classrooms
4. Group = Automation & Deep Tech

Alternative spaces if HBS rooms are full or a different feel is needed:
- Rubenstein House
- SEC (Engineering Building)
- I-Lab

Then put the event on the MBA event calendar (link above; the host signs in).

### 6. Create a Luma
- Make the header from the design system file (see event-host/design.md).
- Create it under the Automation & Deep Tech Luma calendar.

### 7. Food
Decide 3 to 4 weeks out. If catering, finalize 2 weeks out. The budget cap and who approves spending are in the Club info tab; get approval before anything is spent.
- HUDS does catering, roughly $15 to $30 per person. Menu: https://www.dining.harvard.edu/crimson-catering-events/catering. Request form: https://harvarduniversity.tripleseat.com/party_request/43932?hide_banner=1
- Joe's pizza is a good last-minute option.

### 9. Questions or itinerary (1 week out)
Create the list of questions (fireside) or the event itinerary (workshop). Review the speaker's content and planned agenda.

### 10. Bump on WhatsApp (48 hours out)
Short reminder in the same channels.

### 11. Take pictures!!

### 12. Add the event to the event repository
Automation & Deep Tech Events Tracker (Events tab). Set Status to Done and clear Next step. Don't copy RSVPs or attendance; the Luma link covers them.
