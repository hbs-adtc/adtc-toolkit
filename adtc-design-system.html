Rules

How to stay on brand

Crimson #A51C30
Ink #2B2B2B
Body #4A4540
Mono grey #8B857E
Silver #C9BFBB
Paper #F4F2EF
  1. Crimson is the only accent. It marks one thing per view: the date chip, a rule, or the mark.
  2. Two grounds per view, at most. White or paper, plus ink or crimson.
  3. Square and flat. No rounded corners, no shadows. Circles are the only curves.
  4. Archivo talks, Plex Mono labels. Archivo 800 display, 700 headings, 500 body, 600 only for HARVARD BUSINESS SCHOOL. Mono only for metadata, uppercase, joined by a middot, never a pipe or dash.
  5. Speakers in black and white. Background removed for panel and spotlight, straight on white, no tile behind them. Speaker split uses a square B&W crop.
  6. Co-host goes top right under a crimson WITH, logo in black. Never a second club lockup.
  7. One signature per surface. The mark or the lockup, not both twice. On slides the mark sits lower left, never centered.
  8. Flush left, ragged right, never justified.
